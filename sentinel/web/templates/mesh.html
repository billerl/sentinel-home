Invite someone

They need a Meshtastic radio paired with the Meshtastic app on their phone, within reach of the mesh (Meshtastic app 2.6 or newer). Have them scan a code below with their phone's camera, or send them the link: it isn't a web page, it opens in the Meshtastic app. Apps like Messenger open it in their own browser, which shows a blank page: tap ⋯ → Open in browser there, or send it by text message instead.

Chat one-to-one

Adds the base station to their contacts, so they can message it straight away (no waiting for the radios to find each other). Private: only the two radios can read it. Nothing secret is in this code.

🔒 = the two radios have swapped keys, so direct messages are private. Without it a direct message goes out on the public channel (other radios relay it but don't show it). “Direct” radios are heard without a relay. A better-placed antenna (higher, by a window or outside) hears many more.
