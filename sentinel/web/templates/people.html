Vehicles Settings

Family members who agree install the free OwnTracks app, and their phone tells Sentinel where they are. Then you can ask Telegram or Jezebel “Where is Abigail?”, “Who's home?” or “Is Mom at work?”, see a light for each person on the Live page, and get a message when someone arrives at or leaves a place. The phone says where the person is; their car is reported separately, since people swap cars. Sentinel keeps only each person's latest position and their arrivals/departures (30 days).

⚠️ Set the web address under Settings → Remote access first, so phones away from home can reach Sentinel.
