Wi-Fi motion sensors notice people moving between two sensor boards: bodies disturb the radio signal. Every pair of boards is one line through the house, measured from both ends. Each line learns what its quiet room looks like on its own (give it a couple of minutes after a board starts), then shows Movement when the wobble rises well above that. A board with no other board in reach measures the signal to your Wi-Fi router instead.

Boards

Name each board after what it is or where it sits; the lines above are named after the two boards at their ends until you rename them. Flash light makes a board blink white for 20 seconds.
