Routines

Family Settings

A routine does something for you when something happens: when the back yard camera sees a person after dark, turn on the patio lights until there's been no motion for 2 minutes. Make them here, or just say it: “Hey Jarvis, new routine: …” Wi-Fi sensors (the Sensors page) can start routines too, e.g. a message when there's movement inside while nobody's home. or send it on Telegram. Sentinel reads it back and saves it when you say yes.
