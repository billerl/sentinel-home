Routines

Family Settings

A routine does something for you when something happens: when the back yard camera sees a person after dark, turn on the patio lights until there's been no motion for 2 minutes. Make them here, or just say it to Jarvis or on Telegram (“new routine: …”); Sentinel reads it back and saves it when you say yes. The Wi-Fi sensors (the Sensors page) can start routines too, and can be combined: the living room sensor, but not the kitchen one = someone is in the living room.
